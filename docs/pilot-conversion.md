# Pilot conversion — from benchmark to paid contract (Tier 8)

## Penukaran perintis — dari penanda aras ke kontrak berbayar

## The sequence / Urutan

1. **Deliver the benchmark in person** (screen share, 20 minutes). Lead with minutes saved, not features.
   **Serahkan penanda aras secara langsung.** Mulakan dengan minit dijimatkan, bukan ciri.
2. **Name the gap**: "3 of your 20 files had coverage language your team missed on first pass."
   **Namakan jurang**: "3 daripada 20 fail mempunyai bahasa liputan yang terlepas pandang."
3. **Offer the paid pilot**: same 20-file flow, weekly, their adjusters, our drafts. Fixed monthly price.
   **Tawar perintis berbayar**: aliran sama, mingguan, penyelaras mereka, draf kami. Harga bulanan tetap.
4. **Close on paper**: one-page order form. Signature + start date. No legal marathon for pilots under 90 days.
   **Tutup dengan kertas**: borang pesanan satu muka. Tandatangan + tarikh mula.

## Objection counters / Penangkis bantahan

- *"It replaces adjusters"* — "It drafts; your adjusters sign. Time saved per file is measured, not promised."
  *"Ia mengganti penyelaras"* — "Ia merangka; penyelaras anda menandatangani."
- *"Our data?"* — "Processed in-session, never trained on. PII scrubbed in-browser. Audit log on every draft."
  *"Data kami?"* — "Diproses dalam sesi, tidak dilatih. PII disunting. Log audit setiap draf."
- *"Models make mistakes"* — "So do tired adjusters at midnight. Every draft is flagged, cited, and signed by a human."
  *"Model silap"* — "Begitu juga penyelaras letih. Setiap draf dibenderakan, berpetikan, ditandatangani manusia."

## Do not

- Do not discount the pilot to zero — free pilots convert at near-zero rates.
  Jangan jadikan perintis percuma — kadar penukaran hampir sifar.
- Do not mass-email. Three lighthouse carriers, personal follow-up only.
  Jangan e-mel pukal. Tiga syarikat mercu, susulan peribadi sahaja.
